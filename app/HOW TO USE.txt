GearCalc - personal OSRS gear calculator
==========================================

Open GearCalc.exe. Keep the whole GearCalc folder together (the exe needs the _internal folder next to it).
It needs an internet connection for item and monster pictures. Windows 10/11 already include the
Edge WebView2 component it uses.

SCREENS
- DPS: pick a target (each Ocellus phase is its own entry) and compare up to 4 setups. Click a gear slot to
  search for an item. Pick an attack style next to the gear. The star slot top-left is the spec weapon.
  Buffs (prayer, potions, thrall, Slayer task, Mark of Darkness, Charge) apply to every setup.
- Best setup: finds the best gear for melee, ranged or magic on the target. Use as 1/2/3 copies a result
  into a setup.
- Defence reduction: count the special attacks that hit. Stats after specials shows the result. The same
  drains feed the DPS screen.
- Freeze check: freeze chance for every freeze spell, with and without Augury and the Salve amulet (ei).

SOURCES
All calculations use the OSRS Wiki's own DPS calculator engine and data. Extra rules (thralls, freeze spells,
Xarth corpse) come from OSRS Wiki pages and show their source.

Your setups are saved automatically in %APPDATA%\GearCalc.
